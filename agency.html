Frequently Asked Questions for Agencies

Do agencies receive affiliate rev-share?

No. Agencies do not participate in affiliate rev-share. Agencies purchase a White-Label Fleet software license, rebrand the mobile app and portals under their own agency name, and retain 100% of all client billing (both upfront setup fees and monthly management retainers). If you are looking for our 25% affiliate referral program instead, visit the Affiliate Partner Program.

How does remote prompt configuration work without physical phone access?

Your Agency Fleet Dashboard connects directly to the GridAI telephony pipeline and cloud datastore. When you edit a client’s AI receptionist prompt, business hours, or triage criteria in your dashboard, the changes update immediately in the cloud voice engine. The next inbound call automatically uses the new configuration without touching the client’s physical Android phone.

Is the Client Portal really available for both MCAS direct clients and Agency clients?

Yes! The Unified Client Portal (/portal) is a flagship feature across the entire platform. When MCAS direct users log in, they see Missed Call Auto SMS branding. When an agency client logs in (or visits via /portal?agency=yourbrand), the entire portal automatically adopts your agency’s name, logo, primary/accent colors, and support contacts.

Why does physical SIM messaging eliminate Twilio 10DLC A2P regulations?

A2P (Application-to-Person) regulations only apply to virtual VoIP numbers hosted on cloud server platforms (Twilio, Bandwidth, Telnyx). Our solution executes SMS dispatch directly on the client's physical Android handset using native cellular Android telephony APIs. Carrier cell towers (Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile) treat the traffic as authentic Person-to-Person (P2P) cellular communication, making it 100% exempt from TCR registration delays, vetting rejections, and carrier pass-through surcharges.

Can I connect this to GoHighLevel, n8n, Make, or Zapier?

Yes! The Pro Automation engine and Agency Fleet features include bi-directional webhooks. Whenever a call completes or an SMS arrives, an HTTP POST payload is delivered to your CRM webhook with caller name, phone, situation summary, and MP3 recording URL. You can also send HTTP POST requests to our Cloud Relay API to dispatch outbound SMS directly through the client's physical SIM.
