Why does this bypass Twilio 10DLC A2P regulations?

A2P (Application-to-Person) regulations only apply when text messages originate from cloud VoIP servers and virtual numbers (like Twilio, Vonage, or Bandwidth). Missed Call Auto SMS runs locally on a real physical Android smartphone using native cellular Android OS APIs. Carrier cell towers treat it as authentic Person-to-Person (P2P) traffic from an active cellular subscriber, making it legally and technically exempt from 10DLC campaign vetting.

How does the Pro Gateway integrate with GoHighLevel or n8n?

The Pro Gateway edition features bi-directional Cloud Relay APIs and webhooks. When an unanswered call occurs or an SMS arrives, the phone dispatches an HTTP POST payload with caller data to your n8n or CRM webhook. You can also send HTTP POST requests to our Cloud Relay API to dispatch outbound SMS directly through the client's physical carrier SIM!

What happens if my client's AI voice credits hit zero?

If auto-recharge is toggled off and voice minutes reach zero, the Vapi AI receptionist gracefully pauses. The client's phone simply reverts to normal carrier ringing, and their local Android SIM missed-call SMS engine continues operating 100% uninterrupted. There is never any accidental carrier cutoff or disruption to local texting.
