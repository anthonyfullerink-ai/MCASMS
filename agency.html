Frequently Asked Questions for Agencies

How does the monthly pricing work vs. the old one-time packs?

The hardware is still a one-time cost your client handles (~$40 Android phone + $10/mo SIM). The platform license is your monthly recurring fee — starting at $349/mo for 5 seats. This gives you a predictable cost and keeps you on the same billing cycle as your own client retainers. You can cancel anytime.

What are "pooled AI voice minutes" and how does overage work?

Each bundle includes a shared pool of AI voice minutes across all your client seats. The 5-Pack includes 1,250 mins/mo; the 10-Pack includes 2,500 mins/mo. If your fleet exceeds the pool, overage is metered at $0.20/min and billed to your master agency account at month-end via Stripe. Your Fleet Dashboard shows real-time per-client usage and pool consumption.

Can I reassign or transfer a license if a client cancels?

Yes! Inside your Agency Fleet Dashboard, simply click "Revoke Seat" or "Reset Lock". The seat returns to your available quota immediately, allowing you to deploy it to a new client or new Android phone.

Do my clients see my Master Agency Key or Stripe account?

No. You hold the Master Agency Key. For each client, you generate a dedicated child Pro License Key (e.g. MCAS-PRO-...). The client only sees their own business name and activation status on their phone. With the Enterprise plan, your branding replaces all MCAS branding in the setup sheets and portal.

What is the Enterprise / White-Label tier and who is it for?

Enterprise is designed for SaaS-grade agencies managing 25+ client deployments who need their own branded experience: a custom domain fleet portal (e.g. fleet.youragency.com), client setup sheets with your logo, branded customer emails, and negotiated voice pool sizing. Contact our sales team for custom pricing.

How does the phone connect to GoHighLevel or n8n?

Whenever an incoming call goes unanswered, the phone dispatches an HTTP POST payload with caller data to your n8n or webhook endpoint. With "Mute Native Auto-Reply" turned on, the phone stays silent and lets your n8n workflow draft an AI reply and fire it back through the phone's SIM via Cloud Relay!

What SIM cards and phone models work best?

Any budget Android 8.0+ smartphone (such as a Motorola Moto G Pure, Samsung Galaxy A03s, or BLU device) works flawlessly. For cellular plans, prepaid unlimited talk/text SIMs like Tello ($10/mo), Mint Mobile ($15/mo), or US Mobile work out of the box with zero A2P paperwork.
