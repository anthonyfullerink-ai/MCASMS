Frequently Asked Questions for Agencies

Can I reassign or transfer a license if a client cancels?

Yes! Inside your Agency Fleet Dashboard, simply click "Revoke Seat" or "Reset Lock". The seat returns to your available quota immediately, allowing you to deploy it to a new client or new Android phone.

Do my clients see my Master Agency Key or Stripe account?

No. You hold the Master Agency Key. For each client, you generate a dedicated child Pro License Key (e.g. MCAS-PRO-...). The client only sees their own business name and activation status on their phone.

How does the phone connect to GoHighLevel or n8n?

Whenever an incoming call goes unanswered, the phone dispatches an HTTP POST payload with caller data to your n8n or webhook endpoint. With "Mute Native Auto-Reply" turned on, the phone stays silent and lets your n8n workflow draft an AI reply and fire it back through the phone's SIM via Cloud Relay!

What SIM cards and phone models work best?

Any budget Android 8.0+ smartphone (such as a Motorola Moto G Pure, Samsung Galaxy A03s, or BLU device) works flawlessly. For cellular plans, prepaid unlimited talk/text SIMs like Tello (\$10/mo), Mint Mobile (\$15/mo), or US Mobile work out of the box with zero A2P paperwork.
