## 2. Sendblue / SendHub / Cloud Messaging APIs
- **Base Fee**: $150 to $500/month API usage tiers.
- **Deliverability & Trust Friction**:
  - Delivers from VoIP shortcodes or toll-free numbers (800-numbers).
  - Modern iOS/Android spam protection automatically shunts messages into "Promotions" or "Unknown Senders".
  - Homeowners assume toll-free texts belong to lead brokers who sell their inquiry to multiple competitors.
- **Conversion Reality**: 82% reply rate for direct-SIM personal numbers vs. 24% for cloud VoIP shortcodes.
