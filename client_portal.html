⚡ Autonomous 5-Step Execution Workflow

1
Carrier Call Intercept & Custom Greeting
When a call rings into your office line, carrier call forwarding routes it to your AI voice receptionist on ring 1 with your company's custom branded greeting.
2
Real-Time Entity & Address Extraction
The AI asks the caller for their name, problem description, and street address. It actively repeats and confirms the spelling with the caller to eliminate technician dispatch errors.
3
Emergency Intent & Urgency Scoring
The AI checks for critical urgency keywords ("flooding", "gas leak", "burst pipe", "sparking breaker"). If emergency thresholds are met, the call is instantly stamped 🚨 HIGH_URGENCY.
4
Automatic Carrier SIM SMS Follow-Up
Within seconds after hanging up, your office Android smartphone dispatches a real carrier SIM text to the caller with a confirmation message, reassuring them that their ticket is active.
5
Audio Storage & CRM Sync
The audio recording is converted to high-definition MP3 and pushed alongside the full conversation transcript to this portal and connected CRM workflows (GoHighLevel, Zapier, n8n).
