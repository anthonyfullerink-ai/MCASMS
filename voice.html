Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers to common questions about call forwarding, usage billing, and cancellations.

How does call forwarding work with my carrier? ▼
We utilize native telecom Conditional Call Forwarding (*71). When a customer calls your business number, your phone rings normally. If you answer, the call is yours. If you don't answer within 15 seconds (or if you're on another line), your carrier automatically forwards the caller to your assigned AI receptionist number.
How does usage billing and the $10 credit pack work? ▼
You get 15 free test minutes upon activating your $9.99/mo voice receptionist. Ongoing call time is billed at a flat rate of $0.25 per minute in automatic $10 reload packs (40 minutes each). You have complete control: you can toggle auto-rebill ON or OFF anytime in your dashboard. If turned OFF and credits hit 0, your AI receptionist gracefully pauses while your phone returns to standard ringing, and local Android SIM missed-call SMS continues running uninterrupted.
What happens if I cancel my $9.99/mo AI Voice subscription? ▼
You have total freedom and zero lock-in. To cancel, click cancel in your self-serve billing portal. Your AI voice line is safely deactivated, and you dial *73 on your phone to restore standard ringing. Your Android appliance will continue running local missed-call text replies with zero monthly fees.
Can I upgrade from the Base Appliance to the Perpetual Pro Gateway? ▼
Yes! If you purchased the Base Appliance for $49.99, you can upgrade to the Perpetual Pro Gateway at any time for just $249.99 (giving you the exact same $299.99 total cost). Pro unlocks unmetered Cloud Relay API access and bi-directional n8n/Make webhook triggers.
How is this 100% exempt from Twilio A2P 10DLC bans? ▼
Twilio and cloud SMS gateways route messages through virtual VoIP numbers, which carriers heavily filter, block, and tax under A2P 10DLC regulations. Missed Call Auto SMS sends SMS directly through your physical Android SIM card via native Android telephony APIs. Carriers see it as an authentic person-to-person (P2P) text from your real phone.
Can I use my own Vapi / OpenAI keys (BYOK)? ▼
Yes! If you prefer to manage your own Vapi account and telephony numbers, Missed Call Auto SMS features a 100% Free Bring-Your-Own-Keys (BYOK) mode. Simply paste your Vapi Assistant ID and API keys in the Voice Hub settings.
