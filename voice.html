Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on call forwarding, real SIM routing, and trials.

How does call forwarding work with my carrier? ▼
We use standard telecom Conditional Call Forwarding (*71). When someone calls your business number, your phone rings normally for 15 seconds. If you answer, the call is yours. If you don't answer, your carrier automatically forwards the caller to your assigned AI receptionist number. You can cancel forwarding anytime by dialing *73.
Do I get free minutes on signup? ▼
Yes! Every new Autonomous Front Desk ($9.99/mo) activation includes 15 free test minutes automatically credited to your account so you can test calls, configure your prompts, and verify your workflows with zero out-of-pocket minute costs.
What happens when my minutes run out? ▼
If your minute balance reaches 0.0, our auto-pause safeguard safely disables incoming call forwarding to protect you from unexpected overages. Your native SIM auto-SMS continues firing 100% normally. You can reload minutes inside the app with 1 tap at $0.25/min.
How does the Human Takeover Safeguard work? ▼
You are always in total control. The second you manually reply to a customer from your Android phone, our intelligent listener detects your message and pauses the AI for 30 minutes, preventing robotic collision while you close the deal.
Can I use my own Vapi / OpenAI keys (BYOK)? ▼
Yes! If you prefer to manage your own Vapi account and telephony numbers, Missed Call Auto SMS supports Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) mode. You can enter your own credentials directly inside the app settings with zero platform surcharges.
