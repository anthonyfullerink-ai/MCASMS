🚀 Agency Rapid Deployment Blueprint

How to deploy an appliance in 15 minutes for your client and bill them a monthly automation retainer:

Step 1: Hardware (one-time client cost)
Order any $40–$60 Android smartphone (Motorola Moto G, Samsung A-series) and a $10/mo unlimited talk/text SIM (Tello, Mint). Client owns the hardware.
Step 2: Sideload APK
Install MissedCallAutoSMS-Pro.apk on the phone and paste the client's Pro License Key generated from your Fleet Portal.
Step 3: Connect CRM / n8n
In Settings → Outbound Forwarder, paste your agency's n8n webhook URL. Enable "Mute Native Auto-Reply" to eliminate double-sends.
Step 4: Dock & Bill $199–$399/mo
Dock the phone in the client's back office on office Wi-Fi. Set your retainer, connect to their CRM, and collect recurring margin every month.
