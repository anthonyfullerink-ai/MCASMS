Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about Missed Call Auto SMS? We've got answers.
Does the app work when my phone screen is locked or asleep? ▼
Yes! Missed Call Auto SMS runs as a native Android Background Service. Even when your screen is locked, screen is off, or your phone is in your pocket, missed calls are detected instantly and auto-texted according to your schedule.
Why is this 100% exempt from A2P 10DLC registration? ▼
A2P 10DLC rules apply exclusively to web-based Twilio/VoIP API gateways. Missed Call Auto SMS sends SMS messages directly through your physical Android phone's SIM card using your mobile carrier plan — exactly as if you typed and sent the text yourself.
How do app updates work? ▼
Missed Call Auto SMS features an In-App OTA Update Manager. Whenever a new version or feature update is released, the app checks our server and displays a 1-tap download prompt to update your app effortlessly.
What happens if I get a new phone? Can I move my license? ▼
Yes! Each license is bound to 1 Android device at a time for security. If you buy a new phone or upgrade your device, you can reset your hardware binding via our AI Support Assistant or contact support to transfer your key to your new device.
Can I add an AI Voice Receptionist to answer live calls for me? ▼
Yes! While Missed Call Auto SMS is built as a 100% subscription-free SMS appliance ($49.99 lifetime), we offer an optional AI Voice Receptionist add-on powered by Vapi. When your phone rings for 15 seconds without answer, your carrier automatically forwards the call via native *71 to your AI assistant. You can choose our AI Voice Receptionist ($9.99/mo) with 15 free test minutes & metered usage, our Standalone Voice Plans ($29/mo Starter with 45 mins or $89/mo Business with 300 mins), or connect your own Vapi/Twilio credentials for $0/mo (Free BYOK). When the call ends, your phone automatically fires an authentic follow-up text from your real business SIM card!
What is the difference between Founder's Flagship ($49.99), AI Voice Receptionist ($9.99/mo), and Pro Gateway ($299.99)? ▼
Founder's Flagship ($49.99 One-Time) is our pure local hardware appliance built exclusively to detect unanswered incoming calls and reply with instant authentic SMS texts from your carrier SIM card with zero monthly software bills forever.

AI Voice Receptionist ($9.99/mo) is our all-in-one flagship package. It includes 24/7 AI Voice Phone Answering, 15 free test minutes + metered $0.25/min usage in $10 credit packs, 1-tap *71 conditional call forwarding, instant dual SIM confirmation texts, and the complete Pro Automation APK.

Pro Automation Gateway ($299 Perpetual) is engineered for automation engineers, agencies, and high-volume operations. It provides our proprietary End-to-End™ Webhook Gateway (n8n, Make, Zapier, custom CRMs) with 1-Year Cloud Relay API maintenance included to completely bypass carrier A2P 10DLC restrictions.
How does End-to-End™ Integration work with n8n or webhooks? ▼
The Pro Automation Edition features our End-to-End™ FCM Engine. In n8n, Make, or Zapier, add an HTTP Request node sending a 3-line JSON payload with your secret key, phone number, and dynamic message text. Our app receives the payload in real-time—even if your phone is locked and asleep in your pocket—and fires the text directly through your phone's carrier SIM card.
How do I cancel my 3-day free trial? ▼
You can cancel at any time using our AI Support and Voice Assistant right on this website, or directly inside your Missed Call Auto SMS Android App on your phone. Simply click the AI Assistant button or open your app's Account & Subscription Portal, confirm cancellation, and your Stripe subscription will be cancelled immediately with $0.00 charged.
Why does Android display "File might be harmful" or ask to "Install unknown apps"? ▼
This is standard Android OS security notification whenever you download any direct installation package (.apk) outside the Google Play Store.

Because Google Play restricts consumer apps from autonomously accessing telephony SIM hardware to dispatch custom business auto-replies, Missed Call Auto SMS is distributed directly to our customers. Simply tap "Download anyway" in Chrome or Samsung Internet, and toggle "Allow from this source" when prompted.
