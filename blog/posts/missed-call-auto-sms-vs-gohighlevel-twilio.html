The Hidden Nightmare of Cloud SMS: A2P 10DLC

In recent years, major mobile carriers (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile) enacted strict anti-spam regulations known as A2P 10DLC (Application-to-Person 10-Digit Long Code).

If you use a cloud service like Twilio, CallRail, or GoHighLevel to send an automated SMS, your message is classified as commercial application traffic. That means you must:

  • Submit corporate tax documents (EIN / Articles of Incorporation).
  • Pay initial brand registration fees and monthly campaign fees.
  • Wait 2 to 6 weeks for manual carrier vetting.
  • Risk having your messages silently blocked or carrier-filtered if you don't follow exact opt-in disclosures.
